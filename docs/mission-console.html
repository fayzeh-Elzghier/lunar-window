Model boundaries

Ideal fixed-panel direct power with binary Sun-center terrain blocking. No complete battery, eclipse, partial solar-disk, thermal or electrical-loss model.

Sampled passing points do not establish continuity or a physical time margin. Frame/datum alignment and the complete terrain horizon remain unvalidated.

NASA inputs

JPL Horizons supplies calculated Sun/Earth geometry and Sun distance. LOLA local terrain and regional terrain provide a sampled skyline to approximately 300 km.

Moon Trek also supports terrain, lighting and mission planning. ZENITH focuses on an inspectable constrained decision.

Sensitivity: 10% lower output with nine combinations of −1°, 0°, +1° tilt and azimuth offsets; tilt is clipped to 0–90°. The wider test uses ±5°. These are illustrative assumptions, not validated uncertainty bounds.
