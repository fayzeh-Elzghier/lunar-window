03 · COMPARISON VIEW

Two sites. Same scientific limitations.

Site-wide comparisons count original NASA hourly samples; the Dec 20–25 mission screen uses 5-minute JPL samples when the whole request is covered. Neither establishes continuous illumination or a certified RF link.

Why this matters: A sample above the local skyline can still be blocked by unmodelled distant mountains; a sample near the local edge requires finer timing and uncertainty evaluation. Neither is an operational pass.
