# ZENITH human evaluation protocol — results pending

No human sessions or measured time savings are claimed. Automated browser checks are implementation evidence only.

Recruit six English-reading adults unfamiliar with the project. Record prior space/engineering experience without collecting names in the report. Obtain consent to record task timing and anonymized answers. Use the same English-only local build, viewport, reference scenario and instructions. Do not coach; record each request for help and any intervention.

Before sessions, freeze this protocol and the tested Git diff. Allow one minute to explore, then present these tasks in order:

1. Load the demonstration and explain why the original six-hour operation conflicts with demand.
2. Identify the first nominal candidate, determine whether it passes the declared sensitivity exercise, then find the first candidate passing that exercise. State its reserve and the additional delay. Ask whether this proves general reliability.
3. Compare Site01 and Site04 at the same source timestamp; change the date and explain the available cadence. Ask for a 6.25-hour operation outside fine coverage and explain why it cannot be evaluated.
4. Explain why Earth-center visibility does not prove a working ground-station radio link, and why passing samples do not prove continuous operation.
5. Inspect the later candidate and confirm that the original comparison and form are unchanged. Apply it explicitly as a new plan and confirm the preserved latest permitted start. Print the current brief, then change demand and explain why the previous report becomes unavailable.

Record per task: completion seconds from instruction to final answer, success or failure, wrong clicks/interpretations, help requests, interventions, and the participant's explanation verbatim where consent permits. Stop each task after four minutes; mark incomplete rather than guessing success. Use a fixed answer key derived from the shipped reference scenario: original 50.156 W, nominal 12:40 and 100.283 W but sensitivity 82.531 W, later 17:45 with 100.250 W and only 0.250 W reserve, added delay 5h05m. These are model outputs, not hardware measurements.

Predefined pilot success criteria: at least five of six complete tasks 1–3 without intervention; all six distinguish geometric visibility from an RF link and sampled operation from continuity after reading the displayed limitations; no participant mistakes a candidate for certification. Report each criterion separately, even when unmet. Report median and range of completion times, raw success denominators, errors and assistance counts by task and experience descriptively. Six participants do not support population-wide claims or comparative superiority.

For a previous-interface comparison, freeze both builds, randomize/counterbalance order and use equivalent tasks and data. Otherwise make no before/after efficiency claim. Expert lunar-frame, terrain and power review is a separate pending activity.
