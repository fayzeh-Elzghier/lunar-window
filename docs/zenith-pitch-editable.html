WHY THE SAME WINDOW CAN FAIL

01 SUNLIGHT → POWER

A ridge may block the Sun's center above the ideal geometric horizon. Panel angle also affects output.

02 EARTH → LINE OF SIGHT

Earth's center must clear the local terrain skyline. This is geometry, not a verified radio connection.

THE DECISION: WHICH SITE AND START TIME MEET BOTH CONDITIONS?
